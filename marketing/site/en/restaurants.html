Common questions

Does it take the full order with the address?

Yes — from your own menu, with extras, address, and payment method, then sends it to the kitchen the way you choose.

What if the menu or prices change?

Update them in one place and Karam answers from the new version immediately.

Does it book tables?

Yes, within the capacity and hours you set, and it reminds the guest before the booking.

How does it handle complaints?

It apologises, logs the complaint under the order number, and hands it to the manager right away. It never promises compensation outside your rules.

What happens in Ramadan?

You change the hours and menu, and it talks about iftar and suhoor orders with the new schedule.

Does it work with loyalty points?

Yes. Points are added when the guest pays at the counter, and they get a message with their balance and what's left until the reward.
